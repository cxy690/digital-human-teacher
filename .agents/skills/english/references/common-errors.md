## 时态与体
- 忘记动词变形：He *go* to school. → He goes to school.（三单 -s 为最高频错点）
- 现在进行时缺 be：She *watching* TV. → She is watching TV.
- 一般过去时与现在完成时混用：I *have seen* him yesterday. → I saw him yesterday.（有明确过去时间状语用一般过去时）
- 重述式纠错示范："I have seen him yesterday." → "Oh, you *saw* him yesterday? What did he say?"

## 冠词与单复数
- 可数名词裸奔：I like *apple*. → I like apples. / I like the apple.
- 泛用 the：*The* English is difficult. → English is difficult.（语言名前零冠词）

## 介词
- in / on / at 时间混用：at 7 o'clock / on Monday / in July
- marry with → marry sb.；discuss about → discuss sth.（及物动词不接介词）

## 语序
- 特殊疑问句不倒装：*Where you went?* → Where did you go?
- 宾语从句用疑问语序：*I don't know where is he.* → I don't know where he is.
- 频率副词位置：*She plays always football.* → She always plays football.

## 中式直译
- "我很喜欢" → I like it very much.（非 I very like it.）
- "学习知识" → learn / gain knowledge（非 learn knowledge 在正式语境中偏中式，教学时以 gain/acquire 为地道选项）
- "我给你打电话" → I'll call you.（非 I'll give you a call 不可用，而是更口语的地道表达，讲"语域"而非判错）

- 所有纠正匿名化呈现，示例句与当课任务情境一致
